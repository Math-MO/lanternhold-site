Lanternhold: Kingdom Runs

An iOS game: head out on one-thumb expeditions, bring back loot and grow your kingdom.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. The game has no account and no server. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do I turn notifications on or off?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap Notifications, then Lanternhold. Notifications are local: the game schedules them on the device.

Is my data sent anywhere?

The publisher collects no personal data. See the privacy policy.
