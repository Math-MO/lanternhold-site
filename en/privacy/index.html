Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This policy covers the iOS game Lanternhold: Kingdom Runs ("Lanternhold"), published by Mathéo Riba, an independent developer based in France. It is short on purpose, because the game does almost nothing with your data.

In short

  • The publisher of Lanternhold collects no personal data and receives no data from you.
  • No account, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics or tracking tools.
  • The game has no server, and the game's own code makes no network requests.
  • Your progress is saved on your device only.
  • One technical exception: the Unity engine may send anonymous hardware statistics (see below).

What stays on your device

  • Your progress (kingdom, buildings, resources, items) is saved in a file inside the app's folder, on your iPhone or iPad.
  • Your settings (music, sound effects, haptics) are stored in the same place.
  • None of this is sent to the publisher or to any server. Deleting the app deletes this data. If you back up your device (iCloud or a computer), these files may be part of that backup, as with any app; that backup stays under your control.

Notifications

If you allow it (the game asks you; you can say no, and change your mind later in iOS Settings), Lanternhold schedules local notifications: a construction is finished, the daily chest is ready, your workers are idle. They are computed and scheduled on the device. There is no server and no push notifications: no notification token is created or sent anywhere.

Unity engine

Lanternhold is built with the Unity engine. Unity's online services (Analytics, crash reporting, performance reporting, ads, purchases) are all turned off in the project. However, the engine's "hardware statistics" option is on: the Unity engine may send Unity Technologies anonymous technical statistics about the device (for example model, iOS version, processor, graphics chip, memory, screen resolution). This data contains neither your name, nor your progress, nor any game content, and the publisher has no access to it. As with any internet connection, Unity also receives the IP address. This data falls under Unity's privacy policy.

Beta version (TestFlight)

If you test Lanternhold through TestFlight, Apple may share with the developer crash reports, device and usage information (model, iOS version, session length, and so on), and any feedback and screenshots you choose to send. This sharing is handled by Apple, under the TestFlight terms and Apple's privacy policy. The publisher uses it only to fix and improve the game.

Ads and tracking

Lanternhold shows no ads, uses no analytics or tracking SDK, and does not track you across apps or websites. No data is sold, rented or shared.

Children

Lanternhold collects no personal data from any player of any age, children included.

Changes

If the game ever changes how it handles data (for example by adding an online feature), this policy will be updated beforehand and published at this address, with its new date.
